Cheat sheet

How answers are checked

Translations are checked word by word. Russian word order is flexible, so the same words in another order count as right. A one-letter slip in a long word counts as right and is shown. If your translation is right but different from ours, tap My answer was right too.

Finding mistakes

Every sentence has exactly one wrong word: usually an ending (case, agreement, person), sometimes aspect or a missing не. Check what the verb or preposition needs, then whether the endings agree.
