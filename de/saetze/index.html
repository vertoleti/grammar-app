How answers are checked

Translations are checked word by word. In German, word order is grammar (the verb goes second, or to the end), so only correct orders count; common alternatives are accepted. Capital letters don’t matter for the check, and a one-letter slip in a long word counts as right. You can type ss for ß and ae, oe, ue for umlauts. If your translation is right but different from ours, tap My answer was right too.

Finding mistakes

Every sentence has exactly one mistake: usually an ending (case, gender, person), sometimes word order (verb second, verb at the end), haben or sein, or a preposition. Check the verb position first, then what the verb or preposition needs.
