How the test works

Four questions at each level from A1 to B2, easiest first, from all the German grammar lines. You pass a level with 3 of 4 right; the test places you at the highest level you pass without a gap.

If you accept the result, your German level is set to the next one up, and topics at and below your level are marked as known. Your Russian progress isn’t touched.
