How the daily mix works

Each round starts with German grammar mistakes that are due again (they come back after 1, 3, 7 and 21 days). The rest is one recommended question from each line in turn, starting with the lines that have the most topics left to learn.

Your level filter applies here too. Answers count toward each line’s progress, your daily goal and your streak.
