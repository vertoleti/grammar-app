After der, die, das (weak)

mfnpl
Nom-e-e-e-en
Akk-en-e-e-en
Dat-en-en-en-en
Gen-en-en-en-en

After ein, kein, mein (mixed)

mfnpl
Nom-er-e-es-en
Akk-en-e-es-en
Dat-en-en-en-en
Gen-en-en-en-en

No article (strong)

mfnpl
Nom-er-e-es-e
Akk-en-e-es-e
Dat-em-er-em-en
Gen-en-er-en-er

The idea: somebody has to show the case and gender. If the article does it, the adjective takes -e or -en. If there is no article, or ein has no ending, the adjective does it.
