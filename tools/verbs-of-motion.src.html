Cheat sheet

When to use which

  1. Unidirectional (идти, ехать…): one trip, one direction, in progress now or at a moment in the past; also a firm near-future plan (Завтра я лечу в Рим).
  2. Multidirectional (ходить, ездить…): habits, a round trip in the past (Вчера я ходил в кино = went and came back), ability, and movement around a place.
  3. Prefixes add a direction. On the unidirectional stem they make a perfective (прийти, уехать); on the multidirectional stem an imperfective (приходить, уезжать).
  4. Prefixed aspect: perfective for one completed motion (Он пришёл = he is here); imperfective for habits, motion in progress, and visits that were undone (Он приходил = he came and left).
  5. Foot or transport? идти/ходить only on foot; ехать/ездить whenever you use a vehicle, even inside a city.
