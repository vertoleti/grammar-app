How the test works

Four questions at each level from A1 to B2, easiest first, drawn from all the grammar lines. You pass a level with 3 of 4 right; the test places you at the highest level you pass without a gap.

At the end you can accept the result: your level is set to the next one up, and topics at and below your level are marked as known so the coach doesn’t send you back to them. You can retake it any time.
